
WHAT EACH KIND OF PICK IS WORTH, ROUNDS 8-16
scale: points above what that ROSTER SPOT yields for free.
a defence is measured against STREAMING one, a bench man against
the WAIVER WIRE. the spot is the scarce thing, so the spot is the
denominator.

   streaming a defence pays 8.7 a week, 149 a season.
   the best preseason defences returned 135.8 a season.
   so drafting the best defence beats streaming by -13.

ROUND      BACKUP SKILL   STARTING DEF        VERDICT
8-9               +44.0          -12.9   take the skill man
10-12             +32.8          -12.9   take the skill man
13-16             +31.2          -12.9   take the skill man

   and the defence column is generous twice over.
   it credits you the BEST preseason band, but preseason
   ordering picked the better half in only 3 seasons of 5,
   and the bands run 135.8 / 129.5 - not even monotonic. the
   honest expectation is the pooled mean, -16.

============================================================
   THE RULE THIS PRODUCES
============================================================
   a starting DEFENCE is worth roughly -13 whenever you take
   him, so he is a LAST-ROUND pick - not because he is cheap late,
   but because he is no better early.

   a BACKUP skill man is worth +44.0 in rounds 8-9 and +31.2
   in 13-16, so the early bench picks are the ones that pay.

   a starting TIGHT END cannot be settled here. he fills an empty
   slot, so he is worth his projection over the tight end you would
   get at your last pick - a live number, which LiveLateRounds already
   computes on the real board.

   USE THIS TO CHECK THAT TOOL, not to replace it. on draft night
   its ADDS column should put the best defence near or below zero and
   a rounds 8-9 backup well above it. if it prices a defence ahead of
   depth in round 8, it disagrees with five seasons of outcomes and
   the outcomes should win.
