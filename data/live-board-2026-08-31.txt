NEXT CLIFF is the one that decides this. A position's value does not
slide, it steps: the raw rank curve falls off at a few places and is
flat between them. CROSSED means my next pick lands on the far side of
the next step, which is the only version of 'expensive to wait' that
means anything. A smooth odds curve cannot show this - it is monotone
by construction and was measured flattening the real cliff by five
points - which is why the odds are an ingredient here and not the answer.

ADDS NOW is what he adds to the season your STARTERS score, over the
man the wire would give you. VS WAIT is that minus what the best of his
position would add at pick 18 - so it is the cost of waiting, and it is
what to rank on. A position the rules refuse is never priced at all.
