AUDIT: DraftNight and LiveLateRounds against TRAPS.md
Run 2026-08-31, the night before the draft. These are the two tools Justin
will actually have open, so a trap live in them matters more than one anywhere
else in the repo.

CLEARED - and the biggest one is cleared for a reason worth knowing

C13, the hindsight waiver-wire rate. NOT PRESENT in either tool.
  wireRates() - which picks its pool by preseason rank and then averages the
  best REALISED quarter - is called only by PlanBacktest.streamedDefencePerWeek.
  WeeklyStarterValue.forCurrentBoard, which is what both live tools construct,
  builds its wire from PROJECTIONS at the replacement rank instead:
      wire.put(pos, projections.get(ids.get(index)) / 17.0)
  That is hindsight-free. Verified by running both tools with -PhonestWire=true
  and getting byte-identical output: the flag overrides a method the live path
  never calls. The contamination is confined to the backtest.

B8-B11, league scoring. NOT PRESENT. Both tools value men from PROJECTIONS,
  which are built with this league's own settings including the 6-point passing
  touchdown. The 4-versus-6 bug was in how OUTCOMES were graded, which is a
  backtest concern. Nothing Justin sees tomorrow is scored at 4.

A4/A5, keepers and roster size. CORRECT. LiveLateRounds seeds its roster from
  planner.myKeeperIDs() and prints "my roster: 1QB 1RB" before the draft opens,
  so Purdy and Tuten are on the roster and off the board.

F26, Model A out of domain. CORRECTLY SCOPED, and it says so out loud:
  "Round 1 is Model A's game - use DraftNight. This tool is for rounds 8+."

A6, the free wire. DELIBERATE, not a trap. FREE_WIRE defaults on, so an
  unfilled slot is filled from the wire at no roster cost. Turning it off
  (-PnoFreeWire) moves every number a long way - a tight end goes 56.5 to
  146.6 - but barely moves the ORDER, and the current setting is Justin's own
  correction: the bench is fungible, you drop your least useful man and add
  from the wire the same day, so the spot is not an extra cost at the margin.

LIVE RISK - one, and it is latent rather than firing

A1/A2, the quarterback cap. LiveLateRounds HAS NO CAP. It prices all five
  positions at every pick, and with Purdy already held it puts Josh Allen at
  ADDS 77.7 - not zero, because Allen out-projects Purdy and would take the
  slot. The model is not wrong about that. But nothing stops the tool
  recommending a quarterback, and drafting one would leave three on a
  one-quarterback roster.
  Not firing at pick 7: QB survives at 100%, so LOSS IF WAIT is 0.2 and the
  verdict is "wait - he keeps". The risk is late, when backs and receivers are
  thin and a quarterback could top the urgency column.
  THE READ FOR TOMORROW: if LiveLateRounds ever ranks QB first, ignore it.
  LiveBoard, which routes every candidate through RosterRules before pricing
  it, refuses the same pick with a reason.

DraftPlanner.worthTaking counts drafted quarterbacks only and cannot see a kept
  one, so its cap permits three. Harmless in the rounds DraftNight uses it for:
  Model A takes a quarterback only at slots 8 and 9, which are past the starting
  nine and are its indifferent region.

NOT AUDITED

DraftNight's per-pick output. It is interactive - warm in 39.3s, then it waits
  on stdin - so its mid-draft behaviour could not be exercised here without a
  live board. Its rounds 1-7 engine is Model A, audited above.
