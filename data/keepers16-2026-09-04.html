KEEPERS ON THE SIXTEEN-ROUND GAME 2026-09-04 (200 simulated drafts per world, 480 outcome scenarios, weekly starter objective with a defence and a bench)

Values are seventeen-week starter points. SEAT = the owner's keepers phantomed (off the board, no credit, no slot burned), others as declared.

ALONE = that one man kept by himself against the seat (his real round); the top 5 by ALONE plus the declared men form the pair pool. BEST PAIR = the best legal pair in that pool, each priced as a pair.

Every +/- is the standard error of the trial-by-trial difference (the worlds share seeds; the shared draws diverge after the first round a keeper changes, so pairing helps late keepers most). ALONE values do not add: two men kept together free two picks that compete for the same bench.

BEST PAIR is the largest of the searched pairs' means, so its edge over the kept pair carries selection bias on top of its +/-; two pairs inside one +/- of each other are a tie.

The defence wire is the streamed level (WireRateStress: 7.73 a week streamed over 6.98 held). Two keepers priced at one round: the ruleset moves the LOWER-ADP man a round dearer (KeeperPricing); the one case on record, 2025 Jeudy over Daniels, went the other way.
