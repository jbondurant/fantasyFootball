paying anybody IN keepers buys you nothing. -Pkeepers=false prices you the same way he is.

WHAT THIS CANNOT TELL YOU. Sleeper records only COMPLETED trades - 51 across five

is likelier to take. Judge the offer by that column and by what you know about him.
