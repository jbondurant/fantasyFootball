
CAN A LINEUP RULE LEARN IN-SEASON, AND CAN THAT BE VALIDATED?

seasons 13 (2013-2025), boards fantasyfootballcalculator 12-team ADP,
outcomes sleeper weekly, scored under THIS league's settings.
Every prior, level and kappa is fitted LEAVE-ONE-SEASON-OUT.


A. THE ONE FREE PARAMETER, MEASURED

estimate_i(k) = (kappa*prior_i + games_i(k)*observed_i(k)) / (kappa + games_i(k))
kappa is in GAMES: how much evidence it takes to move the prior halfway.

sd_within and sd_between are in units of the position's own season level,
so they are directly comparable across positions and eras. LOO min/max is the
spread of the estimate over thirteen refits, each blind to one season - the
honest error bar on a constant this repo would otherwise carry on a sentence.

The hindsight row is not a strategy. It is the ceiling on every learning rule
that could ever exist here, and it is the bug that once produced a third-round
quarterback and three defences. Anything at all close to it is a hindsight leak.

Seasons needed to detect the honest rule's +5.6 at 95% and 80% power: 193.
Thirteen exist. This claim is not establishable, now or ever.


D. WOULD THE CHANNEL REPRICE A BENCH PICK?

A bench man is worth his MARGINAL contribution - the roster with him minus the
roster without. That marginal is what a draft objective bids. If the learning rule
does not move it by more than the gaps between the men on the board, it cannot
reorder a pick whatever else it is worth.

The gaps this has to beat are on the live board: at pick 127 the objective bids
56.3 for the best free back, 52.9 for the best free receiver and 51.3 for the best
free tight end (BenchValueGap, 2026-08-31). A repricing smaller than those 3-5 point
gaps cannot change which man is taken.


WHY B RESOLVES AND C DOES NOT

THE CLAIM                               effect  SE(season)   effect/SE decisions
B: a flip at week 4 is right           58.493%      1.105%        7.69      6093
C: the season score moves                  5.6         7.7        0.73        13

Both read the SAME thirteen seasons of football and both cluster on season.
The difference is the estimand. A season score's between-season variance is set by
WHICH MEN the roster happened to hold, and no amount of pooling averages that
away. An accuracy is bounded in [0,1], so its between-season variance is bounded
too, and pooling ten thousand decisions inside each season does shrink it.

That is the rule, and it is the transferable part: a new channel earns its place
by a BOUNDED, PER-DECISION claim measured within season. A points-per-season
claim cannot be paid for at n=13 and should never be the price of admission.
