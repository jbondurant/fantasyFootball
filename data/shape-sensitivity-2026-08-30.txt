crude per-season error of that mean: 95. THE TIE BAND IS +/- 125, which is
not that number: it is the measured 80%-power detection threshold for this design
(94 points is bare significance, 125 is what it can catch four times in five).
a shape inside the band has NOT been distinguished from the committed plan by
this evidence. 'paired' below is the sharper test: the same-board per-season
difference, whose error bar is much smaller. 'chg' is how many of the fourteen
drafted men actually changed - a perturbation that changes nobody is a no-op,
not a plateau.

THE KEEPERS ARE NOT IN THIS BACKTEST. PlanBacktest scores the fourteen DRAFTED
men only, so every shape here has to supply its own starting quarterback and
its own second back. Justin does not: Purdy (QB) and Tuten (RB) are kept at
rounds 13 and 12. Any result below that turns on needing a QB early is measuring
a hole his real roster does not have, and should be discounted accordingly.
