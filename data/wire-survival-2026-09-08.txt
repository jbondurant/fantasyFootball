WIRE SURVIVAL  2026-09-08

1442 stretches on the wire across the completed seasons; 869 ended in a claim.
A man is on the wire from the moment he is dropped until somebody adds him.

THE QUESTION: does sitting unclaimed make a man LESS likely to be claimed? If demand is
uniform the hazard below is flat. If some men are wanted by many and most by nobody, then
surviving a day is evidence of the second kind, and the hazard falls.

SAT FOR           AT RISK    CLAIMED    CLAIMED/DAY
under a day          1442         27          1.87%
1-2 days             1413         78          5.52%
2-3 days             1333         51          3.83%
3-7 days             1282        188          3.67%
1-2 weeks            1085        175          2.30%
2-8 weeks             904        319          0.77%

the rate PEAKS at 1-2 days (5.52% a day) and falls to 0.77% a day by 2-8 weeks -
a factor of 7. Waivers run on a schedule, so a man dropped today cannot be taken
until the next run, which is why day zero is quiet rather than uneventful.

SO: surviving the first waiver run IS evidence. A man still sitting after it is in the
group nobody wanted, and the unconditional clearing-price distribution FaabBid uses does
not know that - it averages him in with the Monday-morning handcuff everybody bid on.

WHAT IT CANNOT SEE: men never rostered in this league have no drop to start a spell, so
the least-wanted players are missing entirely. That censoring makes the real decline
STEEPER than this shows - the omission runs with the argument, not against it.
