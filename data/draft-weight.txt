weight: the share of a man's value that is draft value, the rest his pace, that makes that week's trades
most even. CURVE: the same, never rising with games played. uneven: mean |A-B|/(A+B)
at that weight; random: the same sides paired at random, 20 shuffles (real trades should be far more even);
draft and pace: unevenness on either screen alone. vs-rnd: the check - the weight where real trades beat random
pairs by the most. redraft..full refit the weight on one kind of league. same pos: refit on trades where every
man is at one position (RB for RB, WR for WR), with their count - a need at a position cannot explain those.
above line: refit on trades where every man moved is producing above the last starter, so the pace screen's
floor at zero never bites - if the weight on draft fell a long way here, the floor was what kept it high.
A refit on fewer than 30 trades is '-'.
