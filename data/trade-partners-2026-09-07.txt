READ THIS AGAINST THE BOARD. TradeMarket prices each rival's fallback as the best
deal he could find - an assumption of exhaustive search. These rates are what that
assumption is worth: a manager doing well under one trade a season is not running
a pairwise search over eleven rosters, so his real fallback is far below the
computed one and your offer is worth more to him than the board says.
