TRADE MARKET  2026-09-05  (justinb314)
Every size-balanced swap with all eleven rivals, both rosters priced on the same
weekly-starter objective (120 drawn seasons). Only trades BOTH sides gain from are listed:
an offer the other manager loses on is an offer he declines.
THE TWO SIDES ARE PRICED DIFFERENTLY, on purpose. YOUR value is the season PLUS the best two
keeper surpluses on your roster (a surplus being what a man is worth beyond the pick you spend to
keep him, measured at HIS OWN position). HIS value is the season alone, because in this league
people do not give away their keepers and see little in receiving one. Two consequences worth
knowing: buying a cheap keeper off somebody is EASIER than a symmetric model would say, and
paying anybody IN keepers buys you nothing. -Pkeepers=false prices you the same way he is.

keeper value is a tiebreak on a deal that already helps now, not a reason to get worse.

  which is what one-step searching leaves behind.

  AND THE ANSWER TO 'CAN I JUST KEEP TRADING?' - no. Ignoring the floor entirely,
  the chain runs 8 trades and +183.5, but everything past the 4 above is smaller than the
  yardstick's own noise, and it starts churning: it reacquires men it gave away earlier.
  Every trade has to improve BOTH rosters, so the league's total value only rises and is
  bounded by the best possible allocation of a fixed set of players. It must stop, and it
  does. Local maxima are NOT the problem here; the floor is.

WHAT THIS CANNOT TELL YOU. Sleeper records only COMPLETED trades - 51 across five
seasons of this league, and not one refusal - so there is no way to fit what a manager
will ACCEPT. 'His gain' is the honest stand-in: a trade that clearly helps him is one he
is likelier to take. Judge the offer by that column and by what you know about him.
