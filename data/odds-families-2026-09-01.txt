2. WHAT THE SPREAD PARAMETER ACTUALLY FOUND

sigma(r) = r^tau, fitted on all 16 seasons. tau = 0 is the incumbent - one
strength curve, constant spread. tau > 0 says late men are more VARIABLE and
not merely worse, which is the diagnosed miss.

POS    tau frozen  tau refit      alpha  sigma(deep)   log-lik gain
QB          0.000      0.000      0.993         1.00            0.0
RB          0.000      0.573      1.000        10.44            4.3
WR          0.000      0.000      1.001         1.00            0.0
TE          0.000      0.000      0.933         1.00            0.0

tau frozen is the maximum when the curve is left at the incumbent's; it is 0
everywhere, which is a fact about the fitting order - that curve has already
absorbed whatever a spread would explain. tau refit re-estimates the curve at
every tau, which is the fair test. sigma(deep) is how many times wider the
deepest man's spread comes out than the first man's. log-lik gain is IN-SAMPLE
and buys nothing on its own - one free parameter always gains in-sample.
Table 1 is where it has to survive.

|P(r,r)-.5| a man against himself must be a coin flip. antisym  P(a,b)+P(b,a)
must be 1. order  how many pairs where the better rank is the underdog.
monotone  how many pairs where a DEEPER man is given a better chance than a
shallower one against the same opponent, and the worst such jump. strong-ST
is the share of rank triples breaking strong stochastic transitivity:
P(a beats c) >= max(P(a beats b), P(b beats c)).
