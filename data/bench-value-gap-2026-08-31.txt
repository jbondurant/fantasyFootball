MODEL SEES   = WeeklyStarterValue's marginal for adding THIS man to a
               full starting nine. Injury is its only promotion channel:
               a starter who plays and disappoints keeps his preseason
               rank and keeps starting.
MEASURED r8-9= what this league's real rounds 8-9 picks at that
               position were worth over the wire. A different measurement,
               not a target - do not subtract the columns.

The objective bids MORE for a bench receiver than the measured
figure, not less. Losing the bust and boom channels did not make it
stingy, because injury alone - drawn from whole observed player-seasons
- is frequent enough. Ignore the QB row: BenchValue's over-wire is in raw
points, which flatters a quarterback at six per passing touchdown.

What survives for draft night is the ORDERING, which both agree on:
at round 11 a back or receiver is worth three times a defence.
