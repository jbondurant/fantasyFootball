The DEF rows answer a different question from the rest. The nine has no defence,
so a drafted one FILLS AN EMPTY STARTING SLOT rather than sitting behind
somebody - and what it adds is only what it beats the wire defence by. If that
is small, the search has no reason to ever spend a pick there, which is what
the 0.277 rank correlation would predict.

Under season totals a tenth man is worth exactly nothing unless he outscores
a starter outright - that is the blindness that made LiveInsurance report
STARTS = 0% for everyone. Under the starter sum he is worth what he adds in
the weeks he is up and better than whoever else is, which is the thing a
bench player actually does.
