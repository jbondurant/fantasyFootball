weekly-starter objective (240 drawn seasons). Only trades BOTH sides gain from are listed:
an offer the other manager loses on is an offer he declines.
THE TWO SIDES ARE PRICED DIFFERENTLY, on purpose. YOUR value is the season PLUS the best two
keeper surpluses on your roster (a surplus being what a man is worth beyond the pick you spend to
keep him, measured at HIS OWN position). HIS is LOSS-AVERSE on keepers: he takes no credit for one
he receives and feels every point of one he gives up, which is how people here actually behave.
So asking for another manager's keeper is HARD - the ask is priced at what it costs him - and
paying anybody IN keepers buys you nothing. -Pkeepers=false prices you the same way he is.

  yardstick's own noise, and it starts churning: it reacquires men it gave away earlier.
  Every trade has to improve BOTH rosters, so the league's total value only rises and is
  bounded by the best possible allocation of a fixed set of players. It must stop, and it
  does. Local maxima are NOT the problem here; the floor is.
