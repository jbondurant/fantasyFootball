predicts = rank correlation with the outcome INSIDE that tier - whether the
board can tell these twelve men apart at all. A position can look predictable
overall and be noise within every tier, which only means the tiers are ordered
right.

bias = mean ratio. Above 1.0 the tier beats its billing and shrinkage toward
the positional mean is the wrong move - the CENTRE needs moving, not the spread.

bust / boom = share returning under half or over one and a half times billing.
When bust far exceeds boom the pick is a floor and its downside should be
priced; when boom exceeds bust it is a lottery ticket and averaging hides the
only reason to take it.
