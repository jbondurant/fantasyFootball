The crossover never happens, and the reason is the FLEX.

The intuition says that with nobody injured a bench player is useless, so the
pick should fill the empty starting slot - the tight end. That holds only if
the extra receiver really is a bench player. He is not: two flex slots mean a
fourth receiver or third back starts every week regardless, so he is a starter
bought at pick 79, not insurance.

Meanwhile the tight end adds almost nothing even at 0x, because the slot he
fills is not empty - the waiver wire fills it nearly as well for free. That is
the same result the streaming table found, arrived at from the other side.

Injuries still matter, they just do not decide THIS question: they push the
back's value from 44.4 to 53.4 and then back down, because past some point
everyone is hurt including the man you drafted, and the wire fills more of
the lineup either way.

Failure here is games missed only. Bust - a healthy player scoring far under
his projection - would widen the gap further, because a bust starter is a slot
a bench man takes without anyone getting hurt.
