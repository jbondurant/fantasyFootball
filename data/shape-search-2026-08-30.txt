legal    QB 1-2, TE 1-2, DEF exactly 1, RB >= 2, WR >= 3
scoring  PlanBacktest.score, unchanged - real ADP boards, real weekly outcomes,
         lineups set by PRESEASON rank and scored on REALISED points
search   10 random restarts per fold per rule, steepest-ascent hill climb,
         neighbourhood = one substitution or one swap (90 moves from a given shape)
seeds    RANDOM LEGAL SHAPES ONLY. The committed shape is never a start,
         never a hint, never in an objective. It is a scored entrant.

IS THE DIFFERENCE REAL? - paired, season by season
Both strategies draft the same five boards, so the seasons pair up and the
shared variance cancels. This is the sharper test; the five-season means above
are the blunt one.

  SEASON                            2021    2022    2023    2024    2025  mean gap       SE
  LOO by train mean - RUNBOOK        +81    +252     -91    +127    -156       +43       74
  LOO by worst season - RUNBOOK     -149    +448    -298      +9    -249       -48      135
  LOO by minimax regret - RUNBOOK    -125    +125    -310    +126    -327      -102       99

  The honest picker beats the committed shape by +43 a season, give or take
  74. That interval straddles zero, so on five seasons these two are NOT
  separated - and neither is any gap smaller than about 148.

  The floor, held to the same standard: the honest picks never fall below 1906,
  the committed shape falls to 1654 - a gap of +252. But a worst season is a
  MINIMUM over five draws, one observation rather than a mean, so its noise is the
  full per-season spread of 166, not 74. At 1.5 spreads, that gap is SUGGESTIVE, NOT ESTABLISHED.
  Seasons in which each rule's pick beat the committed shape: mean-rule 3 of 5,
  worst-rule 2 of 5. A rule that wins three and loses two has shown nothing.

DEF-LAST DOMINANCE TEST - the one perturbation whose sign is known in advance
Moving the defence to pick 186 shifts every skill pick earlier onto a fuller board,
so the DEF-last roster is ADP-dominant. Preferring the defence earlier can only be
justified by what five particular seasons happened to do.

  HELD   DEF at      train HELD-OUT   train-DL  HELD-DL
  2021   6            2141     2117       1909     1788
  2022   9            2153     1906       2117     1780
  2023   4            2175     2101       1859     1905
  2024   4            2178     2087       1884     1806
  2025   4            2187     1992       1892     2104

  folds whose winner beats its own DEF-last twin ON TRAINING: 5 of 5, by +235 a season
  folds where that preference LOST points out of sample: 1 of 5; mean held-out gain +164

  THE PREDICTION FAILED, and the failure is the finding. The ADP argument said
  DEF-last must win: no opponent here ever drafts a defence, so the same defence
  waits at pick 186 as at pick 42, while every skill pick moves onto a less
  picked-over board. It lost anyway, out of sample as well as in.
  Run -Pshape="<sequence>" to see why: rotating the defence does not merely move
  WHEN you draft - the other eleven drain the board between your picks, so the two
  rosters share almost no players at all. A shape is a lottery ticket over fourteen
  board positions, and an ADP-dominant ticket is not a better-scoring one.
  DO NOT ACT ON THIS. PlanBacktest's defence market is fictional - eleven teams
  that never draft one - so the defence slot is the single least trustworthy
  parameter this instrument has, and it is the one it argues hardest about.
