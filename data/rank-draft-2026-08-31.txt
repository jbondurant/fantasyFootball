Every strategy drafts from the same board at the same picks, so the only
difference between two rows is the order of positions. 'best available by ADP'
is the null hypothesis: never think about position at all.

CAVEAT, and it is not small: the starter-sum sequence came from a model whose
outcome pool was built from these same seasons. Its distributions saw them, even
though this scoring does not. Read its row as flattered, and a narrow win as none.
