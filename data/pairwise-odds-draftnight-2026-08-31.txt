2. DOES THE SMOOTHER HIDE THE CLIFF?

The existing measurement says only ~20% of tier-two backs beat a tier-one back
while tier-two against tier-three is ~42% - a cliff, then a plateau. A fit that
cannot jitter must still be able to show that. Fitted on all 13 seasons:

ISO is the unsmoothed isotonic fit - the data's own shape. LINE is the
one-parameter power law. FIXED8 smooths eight ranks either side everywhere;
LOG.25 smooths a constant width in LOG rank, so it is narrow at the top of the
board and wide in the tail. Read the top row: that is the cliff.


3. IS ONE CURVE ENOUGH? (held-out calibration by region)

The latent form says the whole surface is a function of ONE difference. If that
is false the residuals will not be scattered - they will have a sign that depends
