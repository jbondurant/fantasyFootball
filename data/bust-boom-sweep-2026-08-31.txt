
========== WOULD BUST AND BOOM REORDER A PICK? ==========

800 scenarios, one sampled world shared by every cell, so two
cells never differ by sampling noise. bust rate = boom rate on the
main grid; the asymmetric check is at the bottom.
bust multiplies a man's realised rate by 0.60, boom by 1.60,
and detection after the lag is PERFECT - all three are generous.

REFERENCE  rates at zero: this 2452.6, WeeklyStarterValue 2452.6, gap 0.000
           identical - the channel is the only difference between them
