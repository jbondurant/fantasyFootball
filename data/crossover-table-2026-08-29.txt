smoothed: every query pools pairs within +/-8 ranks, so adjacent rows cannot
leapfrog each other on five seasons of sampling noise. Twelve-wide buckets
did exactly that - a round-10 back could read 10% between a round-9 at 8%
and a round-11 at 13%, which is chunking, not football.

wins = share of pairs where the later man outscored the earlier one that
season; margin = by how much when he did; value = the two multiplied, which is
what the later pick is worth over the man ahead of him.

Read DOWN a column: it should fall smoothly, and now does. Any row that
jumps against its neighbours is still noise, not a discovery.
