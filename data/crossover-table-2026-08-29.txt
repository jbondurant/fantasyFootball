late wins = share of pairs where the later man outscored the earlier one that
season. avg margin = by how much, in the seasons he did. value = the two
multiplied, which is what a bench pick is worth over the man ahead of him.

This needs no model. Both outcomes are in the same season, so the comparison
is direct - which is the answer to how a round-4 bust and a round-10 boom can
be priced together: they already happened together, five times over.
